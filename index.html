Three generations, one family business

Rowland and Ken Wood started Able Plumbing in 1980 out of a small office in Lantana. Today Danny and Krissy carry it into the third generation from our shop on South H Street in Lake Worth Beach.

When you call, you're calling a family business that has answered the same kind of calls in this county for more than forty years.
