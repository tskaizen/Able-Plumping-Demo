New Construction

Underground, rough-in and trim-out plumbing for new homes and additions.

We plumb new homes and additions across Palm Beach County. Builders and homeowners get one crew from the first trench to the last fixture.

Gas piping, water heaters and fixture setting are handled by the same team, so there's no handoff between trades on the plumbing side.

What we handle

  • Underground and slab plumbing
  • Rough-in
  • Water service and sewer connections
  • Gas piping
  • Water heater installation
  • Trim-out and fixture setting

What builders can expect

  • Plumbing for every stage: underground, rough-in and trim
  • State certified plumbing contractor, license CFC044142
  • Licensed, bonded and insured
  • Gas work handled by the same crew
