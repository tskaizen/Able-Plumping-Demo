Remodeling

Plumbing for kitchen and bathroom remodels, from rough-in to the last fixture.

A remodel goes smoother when the plumber shows up on schedule and knows what the tile setter needs next. We handle the plumbing side of kitchen and bathroom remodels, working with you or your contractor from rough-in through the final fixture.

Moving a sink, adding a shower, swapping a tub for a walk-in: we'll tell you what the layout allows before the walls come down.

What we handle

  • Kitchen sink, dishwasher and ice maker lines
  • Shower and tub valves
  • Tub-to-shower conversions
  • Moving vanities, sinks and toilets
  • Fixture installation
  • Permits when the job requires them

Good to know before you start

  • Choose fixtures early. Valve and drain locations depend on them.
  • Add shutoff valves for every fixture while the walls are open.
  • Older homes may need pipe updates to meet current code.
