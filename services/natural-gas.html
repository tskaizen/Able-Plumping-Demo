Natural Gas Services

Gas lines for ranges, dryers, water heaters, grills and generators, plus leak testing.

Gas work calls for a licensed plumber who takes it seriously. We install and repair natural gas lines for appliances inside and out, test for leaks, and check every connection before we leave.

Smell gas? Get everyone out of the house first. From outside, call your gas utility or 911. Don't flip switches or use a phone indoors.

What we handle

  • New lines for ranges, cooktops and dryers
  • Gas water heaters
  • Grills, fire pits and pool heaters
  • Standby generator hookups
  • Leak detection and pressure testing
  • Gas shutoff valve replacement

Signs of a gas line problem

  • A gas smell near an appliance or the meter
  • A hissing sound near a gas line
  • Pilot lights that keep going out
  • Dead patches of grass over a buried line
