Water Heaters

Repair, replacement and new installs for electric, gas and tankless water heaters.

No hot water is one of the most common calls we get. We repair and replace electric, gas and tankless water heaters, and we'll tell you straight whether yours is worth fixing.

If it's time for a new one, we'll help you pick the right size and fuel type for your household, then install it to current code.

Electric, gas and tankless

  • Electric

    The most common setup in Palm Beach County homes. We replace elements, thermostats and full tanks.

  • Gas

    Faster recovery for busy households. We handle the burner, the venting and the gas connection.

  • Tankless

    Hot water on demand and more room in the closet. We install, convert and descale tankless units.

What we handle

  • Element, thermostat and burner repair
  • Full tank replacement
  • Gas water heater installs and venting
  • Tankless installs and conversions
  • Tankless descaling and service
  • Expansion tanks and relief valves

Signs your water heater is wearing out

  • Rusty or cloudy hot water
  • Popping or rumbling from the tank
  • Water pooling around the base
  • Hot water runs out faster than it used to
  • A pilot light that won't stay lit
