General Plumbing

Leaks, running toilets, low pressure, worn shutoffs and repiping. The everyday repairs that keep a house working.

Most calls start small. A toilet that won't stop running, a stain spreading across the ceiling, pressure that drops every time someone showers. We find the cause, explain it in plain terms, and fix it right.

We work on everything from older block homes near downtown Lake Worth Beach to newer houses farther west. If a repair is only a patch on a bigger problem, we'll tell you before we start.

What we handle

  • Leak detection and repair
  • Toilet repair and replacement
  • Faucet and fixture repair
  • Shutoff and main valve replacement
  • Low water pressure
  • Pipe repair and repiping
  • Garbage disposals
  • Hose bibs and outdoor spigots

Signs it's time to call

  • Your water bill is climbing and your use hasn't changed
  • Stains, soft drywall or a musty smell
  • A toilet that runs, rocks or leaks at the base
  • Banging or whistling in the walls
  • Low pressure at more than one fixture
